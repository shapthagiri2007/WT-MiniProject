Context & Objectives

Project Overview, Problem Statement & Objectives

Addressing manual estimation discrepancies through client-side web automation

02 / 08
Challenges in Conventional Billing
  • Manual Calculation Errors: Manual differential reading is error-prone, leading to billing disputes and confusion.
  • Complex Progressive Tariffs: Consumers struggle to verify whether tiered slab rates were accurately partitioned.
  • Zero Pre-Bill Transparency: Consumers receive physical bills weeks later, leaving no means for mid-cycle tracking.
The Proposed Web Solution
  • Instant Real-time Computation: Computes units and slab breakdowns in under 5 ms client-side.
  • Zero Server Overhead: 100% responsive HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript execution without complex backend dependencies.
  • Offline Persistence: Retains past calculation logs in LocalStorage with one-click print invoice support.
01
Automated Calculation

Automatically computes consumed units (Current − Previous) and strictly validates to reject negative or inverted values.

02
Slab-Tiered Engine

Partitions units across 4 progressive domestic tariff slabs (₹1.50, ₹2.50, ₹4.00, ₹6.00) without retroactive penalty.

03
Transparent Invoicing

Generates itemized invoice tables showing consumption per slab, rate applied, subtotal, and print-ready receipt.

04
Persistent History

Saves past calculations to browser LocalStorage, allowing consumers to audit past electricity bills on demand.

Architecture & Stack

System Architecture & Technology Stack

Lightweight 3-tier client-side architecture engineered with modern web standards

Tariff & Math Model

Progressive Tariff Slabs & Mathematical Model

Exact mathematical formulation, tiered rate schedule, and verified benchmark calculations

04 / 08
Core Mathematical Formulations
1. Net Consumption:
U = R_curr − R_prev [where R_curr ≥ R_prev ≥ 0]
2. Progressive Slab Total Charge:
Total Bill = ∑ (Units in Slab_i × Rate_i) for i = 1 to k
*Tariff is progressive: each slab rate applies strictly to the incremental units falling into that tier.
Official Domestic Tariff Slabs
Slab Tier Range (Units) Rate / Unit Classification
Slab 1 0 – 100 ₹1.50 Lifeline Tier
Slab 2 101 – 200 ₹2.50 Domestic Baseline
Slab 3 201 – 300 ₹4.00 Standard Domestic
Slab 4 Above 300 ₹6.00 High Consumption
CASE 1: LOW DOMESTIC CONSUMPTION (80 UNITS)
• Slab 1 (80 u @ ₹1.50): 80 × 1.50 = ₹120.00
• Total Payable Amount: ₹120.00 (Single tier applied)
CASE 2: TYPICAL HOUSEHOLD CONSUMPTION (250 UNITS)
• Slab 1 (100 u @ ₹1.50): ₹150.00
• Slab 2 (100 u @ ₹2.50): ₹250.00
• Slab 3 (50 u @ ₹4.00): ₹200.00
• Total Payable: 150 + 250 + 200 = ₹600.00 across 3 tiers
CASE 3: HEAVY CONSUMPTION (450 UNITS)
• Slab 1: ₹150 | Slab 2: ₹250 | Slab 3: ₹400
• Slab 4 (150 u @ ₹6.00): 150 × 6.00 = ₹900.00
• Total Payable: 150 + 250 + 400 + 900 = ₹1,700.00
